AI in Banking

DBS Put 1,500 Bankers on Agents. The 30% Is a Goal.

THE D*AI*LY BRIEF (beri.net)published 2026-08article8 minworking

DBS expanded an AI-assisted credit-memo tool from a 150-person pilot to 1,500 relationship and credit-risk managers. The much-repeated '30% time savings' number, though, turns out to be a stated goal for the rollout, not a result DBS measured and published from the pilot. The lesson: task-level productivity claims and whole-role capacity savings are different things, and neither means much without a disclosed before-and-after baseline.

Read if you'll be citing a peer bank's AI efficiency numbers in a sales conversation; skip if you just need the headline adoption figure.

Global Banks Adopt Ant's Falcon Forex AI Model

Technology.orgpublished 2026-08article6 minworking

Barclays, Citi, Deutsche Bank, HSBC, Standard Chartered and others are wiring Ant International's FalconTST 2.0 time-series model into named internal platforms — BARX NetFX, Fixed FX Rates, SCALE FX — for FX and cashflow forecasting. It's solid evidence that a non-chatbot category of AI, time-series forecasting models, is actually reaching production at tier-1 banks, not just being piloted. The 93% accuracy and 60% hedging-cost-cut figures being quoted, though, are Ant's own benchmarks, and the adopting banks are also Ant's treasury and technology customers — worth remembering when reading the results as independent validation.

Read if you track how AI model types beyond LLMs are entering bank infrastructure; skip if your focus is generative AI agents specifically.
