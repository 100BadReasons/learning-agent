Why Do Multi-Agent LLM Systems Fail?

Cemri et al. (UC Berkeley) — arXivpaper30 mindeep2025-03

Analyzing over 1,600 traces from seven popular multi-agent frameworks, researchers found most failures aren't model limitations but design failures—poor task specification, agents losing track of role or conversation history, and weak verification of outputs. That means better orchestration design fixes more problems than swapping in a bigger model. The paper offers a concrete taxonomy for diagnosing exactly why a multi-agent demo breaks.
