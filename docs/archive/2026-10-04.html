Model or Harness? An Interaction-Centric Taxonomy for Localizing Agent Failures

arXiv (Scale AI Research)published 2026-07paper35 minworking

A 41-failure-mode taxonomy locates agent breakdowns at specific interaction points — model-to-tool, model-to-memory, model-to-model — instead of treating every bad output as 'the AI failed.' The core finding: most visible failures trace back to scaffolding, tool integration, or context management, not the model's reasoning itself. That reframes who owns the fix.

Conductor: Deterministic orchestration for multi-agent AI workflows

Microsoft Open Source Blogpublished 2026-05article15 minworking

Microsoft's engineers stopped using an LLM to orchestrate multi-agent workflows that have a known structure, replacing it with a deterministic, config-defined routing layer that costs nothing per decision — reserving actual model calls for the agents that need to reason. Their argument: an LLM orchestrator on a known-structure workflow only adds cost, latency, and unpredictability.

At Sibos 2026, Bank AI Agents Fix Payments a Person Still Releases

beri.netpublished 2026-10article8 minworking

Every production AI agent shown by BNY, BNP Paribas, Deutsche Bank, HSBC, and Citi at Sibos 2026 lives inside the exception queue — payment repair and trade-document checking — with scheme-rule validation and a mandatory human release step; none of them let a model release a payment on its own. It also maps which vendor (Cleareye, Traydstream, Conpend) each bank uses for trade-document agents, and notes one vendor's efficiency claims still have no published customer numbers behind them.

AI Customer Communications in Financial Services: The AI Production Paradox

Sinchpublished 2026-07article10 minworking

A survey of 500+ financial-services leaders found 69% of AI customer-service agents have already been rolled back for a governance failure, and the rate is highest — 80% — at the most-governed institutions, because better monitoring catches more problems rather than causing them. The leading rollback cause is PII/data exposure, which is an infrastructure gap, not a model-quality one.
