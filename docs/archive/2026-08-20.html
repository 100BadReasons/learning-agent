Citi moves into more secure agentic AI (Arc platform launch)

Axiospublished 2026-04article5 minworking

Citi's new Arc platform isn't a chatbot or a new model — it's a control layer that lets the bank's 180,000+ employees' existing AI tools run as monitored, auditable, and stoppable multi-agent workflows. It's rolling out to developers first, a sequencing choice other large banks are quietly mirroring.

Read if you're selling into or against a large bank building internal AI governance; skip if your buyer is still pre-pilot.

Anthropic touts AI for COBOL, IBM stock takes a hit

The Registerpublished 2026-02article4 minworking

Anthropic's claim that AI can modernize COBOL rattled markets on the assumption it threatens mainframe lock-in — but IBM posted its best mainframe revenue in 20 years the same month, some of it credited to IBM's own AI code-conversion tools. The near-term effect looks less like banks exiting legacy platforms and more like legacy platforms getting cheaper and easier to keep running.
