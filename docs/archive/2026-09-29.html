τ^τ-Bench: An Environment for End-To-End, Realistic Agent Construction

Sierra / Princeton University (Quan Shi, Keshav Dhandhania, Karthik Narasimhan, Victor Barres)published 2026-09paper40 minworking

This benchmark stops asking whether an AI agent can do a job and instead asks whether an AI system can build the agent that does the job — given the same scattered policy docs, client requirements, production API, and cost budget a real engagement hands you. The best tested setup (Claude Opus 5 under Claude Code) hits only 23.9% success against an 82.2% expert-built ceiling. The gap comes from four specific habits: skimming with keyword search instead of reading the evidence corpus, almost never asking the client clarifying questions, never trying more than one architecture, and shipping the first design that runs.

€95 million left Intesa Sanpaolo's private bank after attackers used an AI-cloned voice and a spoofed executive WhatsApp message to talk two humans in the same authorization chain into moving the money — no system was breached. It's a clean, dated, dollar-figured case showing that dual-authorization controls which don't independently verify the communication channel itself are no longer enough against synthetic-identity social engineering.

Despite the Hype, Few Banks Deploy AI Agents

FinXTech (Bank Director)published 2026-09article6 minworking

Based on Bank Director's 2026 Technology Survey: 72% of bank executives say they use generative AI, but only 30% say their bank is deploying agentic AI at all, and 83% of those are just consuming it inside existing vendor tools (core, CRM, fraud) rather than building anything custom. ConnectOne Bank's deployment on its core provider's platform is one of the few substantive named cases. The honest read: most 'agentic AI' at banks today is a vendor's roadmap showing up in a bank's stack, not the bank's own initiative.

Read before any regional or community bank pitch — it's the reality check on how thin actual agentic deployment still is behind the hype; skip if you already work only with money-center banks running custom builds.
