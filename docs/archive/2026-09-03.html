Agentic AI

Agent Evaluation: A Detailed Guide

Deep (Learning) Focus — Cameron R. Wolfe, Ph.D.published 2026-05article25 minworking

A single benchmark score tells you almost nothing about whether an agent will hold up in production. Real evaluation means inspecting full execution traces, building human-verified 'correct' answers to compare against, and treating your test suite as something that keeps growing every time the agent fails in a new way once it's live. Static, one-time testing is effectively theater.

Read if you need to answer 'how do we know it's working' credibly in a sales or deployment conversation; skip if you just want a product overview.
